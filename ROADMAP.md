# LUMORA X Roadmap

## Stage 1 — Base camera (this project)

Camera permission, native preview, physical-lens discovery, front/back switching, tap focus, exposure bias, manual ISO/shutter/focus, flash, capture, MetalPetal/Core Image preset processing, Photo Library save, settings, logging, and honest error states.

## Stage 2 — Professional controls

Manual white balance, AE/AF lock, histogram, focus peaking, zebra, RAW capability checks, and orientation/device test matrix.

## Stage 3 — Preset engine

Metal-based live preview, LUT import with license tracking, tone curves, custom preset editor, intensity control, color-managed export, and preview/output parity tests.

## Stage 4 — Image quality

Scene-aware luminance/chroma denoise, adaptive sharpening, lens correction, highlight/shadow recovery, face-aware skin protection using Vision, and artifact regression tests.

## Stage 5 — Multi-frame capture

Burst acquisition, motion scoring, reference-frame selection, alignment, ghost-resistant merge, HDR, and Night Fusion. All modes must degrade safely to a sharp single frame.

## Stage 6 — Optimization

Instruments profiling, Metal System Trace, memory-pressure handling, thermal policies, pixel-buffer pooling, iPhone 12 performance budgets, battery testing, and 20-shot stress tests.

## Stage 7 — Release

Final icon and screenshots, localization, privacy policy, accessibility audit, complete licenses, App Store metadata, TestFlight, device lab validation, and production signing.
